01 / WEB PLATFORM NEPAL / 01

Bike Rental Management System

A web-based platform concept to modernize bike rentals in Nepal. It brings bookings, payments, inventory, and customer support into one place, with live availability and an admin panel for managing bikes, reservations, and customer information.

Planned with an iterative Agile approach, using user feedback to guide improvements and support future growth.

02 / LEARNING SPACE EDUCATION / 02

Course Management System

A centralized learning platform for instructors and students. Instructors can organize courses, share materials, assign work, run quizzes, and track progress. Students can access lessons, submit assignments, take assessments, and receive feedback in one place.

Designed to make course organization and communication clearer for both learners and instructors.

03 / UI·UX CONCEPT FINANCE / 03

Money Management System

A mobile finance and digital wallet concept inspired by services such as eSewa, Khalti, and mobile banking apps. The design explores sending and receiving money, payments, top-ups, bill payments, and everyday money management.

Design prototype only: this project focuses on interface design and prototyping; it is not a developed or connected banking app.
